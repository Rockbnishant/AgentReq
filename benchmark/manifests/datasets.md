## Provenance notes

PROMISE NFR is reported in the literature as 625 requirements from 15 projects. PURE contains 79 public requirements documents and 34,268 sentences. The traceability classification benchmark provides preprocessed requirements for five traceability-link-recovery datasets.

Before publishing experimental results, record the exact downloaded archive checksum, preprocessing commit, project split, random seed, and license in this manifest or an accompanying machine-readable manifest.
